FAQThe ones I hear most

Before you ask.

Q.1Will my website go down during the transfer?

No. The new site is built while the old one keeps running, and the switch is a DNS change — visitors never see a gap.

Q.2My shop has hundreds of products. Can you really move it?

Yes — catalogs move over programmatically, not by hand, so hundreds of products with photos, prices and categories is routine. We've rebuilt full grocery and wholesale catalogs this way.

Q.3Will Google still find me after the move?

Yes. Every old page address is redirected to its new home and your titles and descriptions carry over, so your rankings follow the site instead of resetting.

Q.4Do I still need my old hosting afterwards?

Usually not — that's the point. Once the new site is live and verified, the old hosting plan can be cancelled, and that saving alone often pays for the move.

Q.5What if I'm stuck on Wix or GoDaddy specifically?

Those builders have extra traps — no export button, locked domains — and we have a whole playbook for them. Read about website rescues.
