    QuestionsThe ones I hear most

    Straight answers.

    Q.1How fast is it?

    Most websites are live within days. Online stores and web apps take a little longer, and you get the timeline before we start.

    Q.2What does it cost?

    Fair prices, told in writing before we start. You pay the balance only after you have seen your design, and any running costs are told up front.

    Q.3What if I need a change after launch?

    Send it from your phone at bhwebs.com/support: typed, a photo or a voice message. Usually done within 24 hours, and small edits stay free.

    Q.4Can I update it myself?

    Yes. Prices, photos, hours and products are in a plain-language admin you run yourself.

    Q.5Who owns the website?

    You do. Your domain, your payment account and your admin password are in your name. If you ever leave, you leave with all of it.

    Q.6Does it work on old iPads and filtered phones?

    Yes. Every site is built and tested for older iPads and filtered devices, so your customers can always see it.

    Q.7Can you move my existing website?

    Yes. We move WordPress and Wix sites to a faster, safer setup without changing how they look. How a move works.
